Microcement

One continuous surface. No grout. No seams. No end.

A polymer-modified cementitious finish troweled by hand in layers only a few millimetres thick — floor flowing up the wall, into the shower, over the vanity, down the stair, without a single joint to interrupt it. It can often go directly over sound existing tile, so a room transforms without demolition.

05

Venetian plaster & limewash

Stone, returned to stone.

Slaked lime and marble dust, laid in whisper-thin coats and burnished with a steel trowel until it shines like polished rock. The Romans knew it. The Venetians perfected it. As it cures, lime draws carbon dioxide from the air and slowly becomes limestone again — which is why it breathes, why its alkalinity resists mould, and why it grows more beautiful with age rather than less.

Stucco Veneziano

Polished to a deep, cool, marble-like gloss.

Marmorino

Matte to satin. Mineral, velvety, quietly luminous.

Limewash

Brushed, cloudy movement. Soft as weather on old walls.

Tadelakt

Moroccan lime, stone-polished and soaped. Water-resistant, seamless, sensual.

Yakisugi

Fire as a finish.

The Japanese art the West calls shou sugi ban: timber charred until its surface turns to silver-black alligator scale. The char helps the wood shed water and weather and resist insects and rot — beauty that is also protection.

Our signature: luminous yakisugi. Photoluminescent mineral set deep into the charred grain drinks the daylight and returns it after dark — a façade, a fence, a wall that glows softly into the night.
